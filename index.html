The Forge

0 / 16

Each spin rolls three side-by-side reels: one trait and two SRD monsters, with at least one option guaranteed to have that trait. Choose your favorite monster before keeping the trait, then press Spin the Forge to start the next round. Your boss commands eight CR 2–7 minions. Boss HP starts at 100, plus the HP rolled in the HP slot and the Constitution adjustment. You have five shared respins for the entire forge. Respin both monster choices without changing the trait, or respin the trait while keeping the two monster choices.

How the challenge works

Your boss automatically fights parties at levels 5–20, with 200 battles per level (3,200 total). Winning at least 65% of battles defeats that level. Your result is the highest level defeated, with a full breakdown below.

This is an original D&D-inspired arcade game, not an official D&D rules simulator. All 322 SRD 5.2.1 monster profiles are bundled directly into the game. No external download is required. You can also save a standalone copy for offline play. Combat abilities are simplified for Arcade play.
