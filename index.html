How to Play

  1. Spin the Forge. Get two monster-and-trait offers.
  2. Pick your favorite. Tap a monster, then select Keep Selected Monster to add its trait to your boss.
  3. Use respins wisely. You have 5 total to change monsters, traits, or both. All three respin buttons share the same pool.
  4. Fill all 16 trait slots. Look for strong traits and combinations that activate synergies. Your boss also commands five CR 2–7 minions.
  5. Face the party. Your finished boss automatically battles the Fighter, Wizard, Cleric, Rogue, and Paladin at levels 5–20. Fight one battle per level, starting at level 5. Win to advance; the first loss or draw ends the gauntlet. Defeat level 20 to win the challenge.
  6. Climb the leaderboard. Check your personal and global rankings after the challenge.

Tip: Your boss starts with 200 HP, plus its HP trait and Constitution bonus. On the final spin, both offers compete for the last remaining trait.

How the challenge works

Your boss enters a sudden-death gauntlet against parties from levels 5–20. Each level has exactly one battle. Win to advance; a loss or draw immediately ends the run. Defeat level 20 to complete the gauntlet.

This is an original D&D-inspired arcade game, not an official D&D rules simulator. All 322 SRD 5.2.1 monster profiles are bundled directly into the game. No external download is required. You can also save a standalone copy for offline play. Combat abilities are simplified for Arcade play.
