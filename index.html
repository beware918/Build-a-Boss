How the challenge works

Your boss automatically fights parties at levels 5–20, with 200 battles per level (3,200 total). Winning at least 65% of battles defeats that level. Your result is the highest level defeated, with a full breakdown below.

This is an original D&D-inspired arcade game, not an official D&D rules simulator. Monster stats are sourced from SRD 5.2.1; once loaded, they are cached on this device for offline play. You can also download a fully standalone copy with all 322 monsters embedded. Combat abilities are simplified for Arcade play.
